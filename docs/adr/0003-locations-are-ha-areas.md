# Locations are HA areas, no manual camera map

A camera's Location is the HA Area the camera's device is assigned to, read live at trigger time; the integration keeps no camera-to-location map of its own and stores no area in its configuration. Rationale: areas are already maintained in HA, and a second map would drift from reality. Consequences: a Location can only be an existing HA area (a "garden" area must be created before garden cameras track); the config flow rejects cameras that are not in an area, and a camera whose area is later removed is skipped with a log line when its trigger fires, rather than failing setup; moving a camera to another area or renaming an area takes effect without a reload and changes tracker state text. To stay unambiguous, tracker state carries the area display name while an attribute carries the area ID, since two areas may share a display name.

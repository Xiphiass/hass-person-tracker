# Presence re-check before a sighting expires

A Person who stays put (sofa, desk, dinner table) fires no new trigger edge, and many person-detected sensors stay ON the whole time someone is in view, so under ADR 0004 alone they expire to "unknown" while in plain sight. Shortly before a sighting's TTL runs out (60 s, capped at half the TTL), the Person's last camera is snapshotted once more and Identification is asked about that Person only, with only their reference images and a prompt stating that anyone else in the frame counts as unknown. A confident confirmation refreshes the sighting (and so schedules the next re-check); a miss, a low confidence or a failed run changes nothing and the TTL expires as before (no retry, Issue #5). People last seen on the same camera whose re-checks fall within the lead share one run. A re-check ignores the camera's cooldown but claims it; it is skipped for a Person whose authoritative tracker reports them away or who has no reference images, and it never saves an Unknown snapshot. It is a global setting, on by default. Cost: at most one AI call per TTL per Person, and only while that Person stays in view. Refines ADR 0004: last-known-wins with TTL still holds, the TTL just gets one chance to be confirmed. Rejected alternatives: re-running the full identification (every reference image on every re-check, more tokens for a narrower question), checking every camera in the area (more calls, and "still where we last saw them" is what we need), and periodic polling of all cameras (cost regardless of whether anyone is there).

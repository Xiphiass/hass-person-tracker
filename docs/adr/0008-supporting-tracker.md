# Supporting tracker: camera leads, supporting tracker falls back

A Person may have one optional Supporting tracker: a room-level location source of lower trust than a camera sighting, such as a BLE tracker (Bermuda, ESPresense). It is configured as two separate, optional entities, one whose state names an HA area and one whose state names an HA floor, so any integration fits. Their states are resolved to HA areas and floors through the registries (an `area_id`/`floor_id` attribute first, then ID, name and aliases), keeping ADR 0003's rule that Locations are HA areas. Ranking: the authoritative tracker can still only demote, and when it reports the Person away everything is unknown, the supporting tracker included. The camera sighting leads while it is current. The supporting tracker leads once the sighting has expired (or before any sighting): the tracker stays `home` with the supporting area and floor, and a `source` attribute names which source leads (`camera`, or the supporting area entity's ID). A supporting floor without a supporting area gives no presence, because a floor alone is too coarse. The camera sighting also stops leading early if the supporting area changed after the last sighting and has held the new area for a settle period: the Person moved somewhere the cameras did not see. A supporting tracker that has disagreed since before the sighting is the one the camera just proved wrong, so the camera keeps leading. `last_location`, `last_seen` and `last_camera` keep describing the last camera sighting. Two refinements of ADR 0007 follow. A re-check is skipped while the supporting tracker leads after a move, because it would snapshot the camera the Person left. Only the moved Person is dropped from a shared re-check, and the camera sighting then expires without a retry. The trade-off: if the supporting tracker is wrong about the move, the re-check that would have caught it does not run; the next camera trigger corrects it. A settled move into an area that has cameras, with no newer camera sighting, triggers one move check on every camera of that area about that Person only, so the camera can confirm the move. It is worded like a re-check but asks whether the Person is here, leaves out cameras in cooldown (their recent run already answered) and claims the cooldown of the others, never saves an Unknown snapshot, and is skipped when the authoritative tracker reports the Person away or a current camera sighting already places them there, or when the Person has no reference images. Persons who move into the same area at the same time share one run per camera. A confirmation is a normal sighting without the corroboration boost (ADR 0009), so the camera leads again; a miss or a failed run changes nothing and is not retried. A global option turns the move check off. Refines ADR 0004 and ADR 0006: the state is `home` while a camera sighting or the supporting tracker places the Person at home. Rejected alternatives: several supporting trackers ranked against each other (more configuration and ranking rules for a source that is unreliable anyway); letting supporting-tracker agreement refresh a sighting in place of a re-check (it would keep a camera sighting alive on BLE alone, and the fallback already reports that case truthfully); and "the camera leads while current" with no move exception (the tracker would show the old room for up to a whole TTL after the Person walked into a room without a camera).

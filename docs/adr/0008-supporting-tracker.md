# Supporting tracker: camera leads, supporting tracker falls back

A Person may have one optional Supporting tracker: a room-level location source of lower trust than a camera sighting, such as a BLE tracker (Bermuda, ESPresense). It is configured as two separate, optional entities, one whose state names an HA area and one whose state names an HA floor, so any integration fits. Their states are resolved to HA areas and floors through the registries (an `area_id`/`floor_id` attribute first, then ID, name and aliases), keeping ADR 0003's rule that Locations are HA areas. Ranking: the authoritative tracker can still only demote, and when it reports the Person away everything is unknown, the supporting tracker included. The camera sighting leads while it is current. The supporting tracker leads once the sighting has expired: the tracker stays `home` with the supporting area and floor, and a `source` attribute names which source leads. A supporting floor without a supporting area gives no presence, because a floor alone is too coarse. The camera sighting also stops leading early if the supporting area changed after the last sighting and has held the new area for a settle period: the Person moved somewhere the cameras did not see. A supporting tracker that has disagreed since before the sighting is the one the camera just proved wrong, so the camera keeps leading. `last_location`, `last_seen` and `last_camera` keep describing the last camera sighting. Two refinements of ADR 0007 follow. A re-check is skipped while the supporting tracker leads after a move, because it would snapshot the camera the Person left. A settled move into an area that has cameras, with no newer camera sighting, triggers one move check on that area's cameras about that Person only (re-check style, cooldown respected, no Unknown snapshot), so the camera can confirm the move. A confirmation is a normal sighting; a miss changes nothing. Refines ADR 0004 and ADR 0006: the state is `home` while a camera sighting or the supporting tracker places the Person at home. Rejected alternatives: several supporting trackers ranked against each other (more configuration and ranking rules for a source that is unreliable anyway); letting supporting-tracker agreement refresh a sighting in place of a re-check (it would keep a camera sighting alive on BLE alone, and the fallback already reports that case truthfully); and "the camera leads while current" with no move exception (the tracker would show the old room for up to a whole TTL after the Person walked into a room without a camera).

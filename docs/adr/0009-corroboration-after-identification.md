# Supporting tracker corroborates after identification

The supporting tracker (ADR 0008) can raise the confidence of an Identification hit but never lowers it, and it never reaches the AI. Once the AI has answered, a hit is compared with the Person's supporting tracker: a matching area adds a larger boost, otherwise a matching floor adds a small one. The boosts never add up, the result is capped at 1.0, and only then is the hit compared with the minimum confidence. The boost applies to detection runs and re-checks, but not to move checks: there the supporting tracker already chose both the camera and the Person, so boosting the answer would make the confirmation circular. A disagreeing supporting tracker changes nothing, because BLE noise should not discard a good camera hit, and the camera still leads under ADR 0008's rules. Rationale: the camera must stay an independent check on the supporting tracker. Hinting the supporting tracker's area in the prompt ("Christian's phone is near this camera") would bias the AI towards the very answer it is supposed to confirm. Rejected alternatives: prompt hints (bias, as above); lowering the minimum confidence when the supporting tracker agrees (the same effect, expressed less clearly per hit); and a penalty for disagreement (it lets an unreliable source veto a reliable one).
